NLP Pipeline & Feature Extraction

Raw unstructured text undergoes strict Natural Language Processing:

  • Regex Sanitization: Non-alphabetic characters and noise stripped
  • Standardization: Lowercase normalization
  • Stopword Removal: Filtered via NLTK English stopwords
  • Lemmatization: NLTK WordNet Lemmatizer reduces words to dictionary roots
  • Vectorization: TF-IDF n-grams (1,2) with 5,000 max features
